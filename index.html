Frequently asked questions

What aircon services does SoCool provide?

SoCool lists general servicing, chemical wash, steam wash, chemical overhaul, repairs, troubleshooting, gas top-up, installation and relocation services.

Do you provide commercial aircon services?

SoCool lists commercial systems including VRV, VRF, ducted and chilled-water systems. Contact the team to confirm the required scope and availability.
