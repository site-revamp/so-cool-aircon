# SoCool Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

SoCool Pte Ltd provides aircon servicing, cleaning, repairs, installation, and commercial HVAC maintenance in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps homeowners and businesses compare services, understand what they include, and request an appointment.

## Main Improvements

1. **Clarify prices and service inclusions.** The existing service descriptions give conflicting information about gas top-ups. The redesign proposes readable tables for normal servicing, chemical wash, steam wash, and chemical overhaul, with one-time rates and yearly contracts shown separately. Each option would explain whether gas top-ups and blower-wheel removal are included or chargeable, along with any extra fees or conditions for higher-capacity units.
2. **Make services easier to find.** Overlapping descriptions and inconsistent terminology make the current site harder to navigate. Clear sections for residential services, commercial HVAC, repairs, and maintenance contracts would give visitors distinct starting points. Shorter descriptions and prominent appointment links would connect each section to the next step.
3. **Make service quality and booking easier to assess.** The redesign proposes genuine reviews linked to the original Google profile, technician and project photography, and a clear explanation of the diagnostic process. The advertised 90-day workmanship warranty would appear with confirmed coverage and exclusions. A simpler mobile appointment form and visible Call and WhatsApp actions would make enquiries easier.

## Design Improvements

The proposed layout uses SoCool’s red, black, and white palette, with red highlighting appointment actions. Consistent headings and pricing tables would make service categories, charges, and contract terms easier to scan.
